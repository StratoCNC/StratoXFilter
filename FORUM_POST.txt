======================== ENGLISH VERSION ==============================

[b][size=150]Free tool: cancel your CNC's hidden nonlinear positioning error (measure → map → correct the G-code)[/size][/b]

You calibrated steps-per-mm. You set backlash comp. Homing repeats fine. And yet an 800&nbsp;mm feature comes out 799.8, an 1100&nbsp;mm one comes out 1101.2, and a "round" bore is slightly egg-shaped.

That's [b]nonlinear, position-dependent[/b] error — a smooth arch of geometric error along the axis (rack/mesh, pinion, rail alignment). It is [b]not backlash[/b] and [b]not[/b] a steps-per-mm error, so neither of those fixes it. On rack-and-pinion gantries it's often [b]different in each direction[/b] too.

[img][IMG_URL][/img]
[i]The measured error map — two curves, one per travel direction. Peak ~1.25&nbsp;mm here.[/i]

I wrote a small, free tool ([b]StratoXFilter[/b], MIT) that fixes it in software: it warps the G-code against a measured error map, per direction, on X/Y/Z. It also splits arcs into short lines (so corrected arcs don't break the controller), has optional backlash comp, a single-move MDI helper, and an automatic self-check on every run. A built-in [b]map builder[/b] turns your Excel/CSV measurement table straight into the map (median across passes) — no hand-editing. Input is a .TAP/G-code file; output is a corrected file for your controller. Machine- and CAM-agnostic — you just measure your machine and load the map.

[b]Full write-up[/b] (problem → how to measure with a linear scale → building the maps → results): [url=[ARTICLE_URL]]read here[/url]
[b]Download / source[/b] (Windows .exe + Python): [url=[DOWNLOAD_URL]]here[/url]

Verify with a dry run before cutting — it rewrites G-code. Questions, results and improvements welcome.

[b][size=150]Безплатен инструмент: премахни скритата нелинейна позиционна грешка на CNC-то (измери → картографирай → коригирай G-кода)[/size][/b]

Калибрирал си стъпки-на-мм. Настроил си backlash. Homing-ът повтаря. И пак — 800&nbsp;мм елемент излиза 799.8, 1100&nbsp;мм излиза 1101.2, а "кръгъл" отвор е леко яйцевиден.

Това е [b]нелинейна, позиционно-зависима[/b] грешка — плавна дъга от геометрична грешка по оста (рейка/захапка, пиньон, центровка на водачите). [b]Не е backlash[/b] и [b]не е[/b] грешка на стъпки-на-мм, затова нито едното не я оправя. При рейка-пиньон гентри често е и [b]различна в двете посоки[/b].
